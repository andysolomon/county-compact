# Issue #1 — Georgia counties abolished before 1 February 1942

Scope: every Georgia county that once existed and no longer existed on the campaign reference date, so that none of them is mistaken for a 1942 start. Source of record: Newberry *Atlas of Historical County Boundaries*, Georgia Individual County Chronologies (`https://publications.newberry.org/ahcb/documents/GA_Individual_County_Chronologies.htm`) and County Index, corroborated by the 1940 Census (Vol. I, Georgia, Table 3 and Table 4 footnotes) and the Georgia Archives Virtual Vault county-records index. Quotations are verbatim.

The Newberry County Index for Georgia lists exactly three entries marked extinct — `BOURBON (extinct)`, `CAMPBELL (extinct)`, `MILTON (extinct)` — plus `WALTON (original, extinct)`, the colonial parishes (`Christ Church Parish`, `St. Andrew … St. Thomas Parish`), and cross-references for the renames above. No other Georgia county was ever abolished. **Campbell and Milton are the only counties whose territory lies inside modern Georgia and that were abolished after the county network was complete; both are inside modern Fulton.**

## Campbell County (extinct)

Newberry individual chronology, entry `CAMPBELL (extinct)`:

> 20 Dec 1828 — CAMPBELL (extinct) created from CARROLL, COWETA, DEKALB, and FAYETTE. (Ga. Laws 1828, pp. 56-58; Dawson's Compilation, 139-140)
> 08 Nov 1870 — CAMPBELL (extinct) lost to creation of DOUGLAS, and gained from FAYETTE [act passed 17 October 1870; took effect 8 November 1870]. (Ga. Laws 1870, no. 5/pp. 13-16)
> 24 Aug 1872 — CAMPBELL (extinct) lost to FULTON (lots 55, 56, 57, 58, 84, 85; 14th dist.). (Ga. Laws 1872, July adj. sess., loc. & priv., no. 270/p. 397)
> 01 Jan 1932 — CAMPBELL (extinct) lost all territory to FULTON; CAMPBELL eliminated. Act passed 9 August 1929; took effect 1 January 1932. (Ga. Laws 1929, p. 551)

Newberry Comprehensive Database row: `NAME=CAMPBELL (extinct)`, `ID=gas_campbell`, `START_N=19320101`, `CREATION_OR_CHANGE="CAMPBELL (extinct) lost all territory to FULTON; CAMPBELL eliminated. Act passed 9 August 1929; took effect 1 January 1932."`, `CITATION="(Ga. Laws 1929, p. 551)"`. No Campbell version has `END_N ≥ 19420201`.

1940 Census, Vol. I, Georgia, Table 3 footnote (OCR text layer, page 241): "State totals for 1930 and 1920 include population (9,903 in 1930; 11,709 in 1920) of Campbell County and population (6,730 in 1930; 6,885 in 1920) of Milton County annexed to Fulton County in 1932." Table 4 footnote 25 (page 244): "FULTON—County totals for 1930 and 1920 are exclusive of population (9,903 in 1930; 11,709 in 1920) of Campbell County; population (6,730 in 1930; 6,885 in 1920) of Milton County; and population (1,568 in 1930; 1,316 in 1920) of district 845, Cobb County, including Roswell town, annexed to Fulton County, in 1932".

Georgia Archives Virtual Vault, collection "Index to County Records on Microfilm (No Images)": item `Campbell (Fulton)`, identifier `CC021`, source "County Records Microfilm Index, Georgia Archives" (`https://vault.georgiaarchives.org/digital/collection/countycards/id/5`). The archives index Campbell's surviving records under Fulton.

Cross-reference (not relied on): Wikipedia *Campbell County, Georgia* — seat Fairburn (originally Campbellton); "The legislation creating the merger was enacted on August 9, 1929". New Georgia Encyclopedia *Fulton County*: "On January 1, 1932, Fulton County nearly tripled in size, to 528 square miles, when Campbell and Milton counties were added to Fulton."

**Status for the game:** must not appear as a 1942 county. Its territory is the south-western lobe of modern Fulton (Fairburn, Palmetto, Union City, Campbellton area). The 1870 portion north-west of the Chattahoochee is modern Douglas and is already a separate 1942 county.

## Milton County (extinct)

Newberry individual chronology, entry `MILTON (extinct)`:

> 01 Feb 1858 — MILTON (extinct) created from CHEROKEE, COBB, and FORSYTH. Act passed 18 December 1857; took effect 1 February 1858. (Ga. Laws 1857, pub., no. 21/pp. 36-38)
> 19 Dec 1859 — MILTON (extinct) gained from DEKALB and GWINNETT. (Ga. Laws 1859, loc. & priv., no. 249/p. 271)
> 08 Dec 1860 — MILTON (extinct) gained from FORSYTH. (Ga. Laws 1860, loc. & priv., no. 128/p. 138)
> 01 Jan 1932 — MILTON (extinct) lost all territory to FULTON; MILTON eliminated. Act passed 30 July 1931; took effect 1 January 1932. (Ga. Laws 1931, p. 527; "Milton County," 2009)

Newberry Comprehensive Database row: `NAME=MILTON (extinct)`, `ID=gas_milton`, `START_N=19320101`, `CITATION="(Ga. Laws 1931, p. 527; \"Milton County,\" 2009)"`. The secondary citation "Milton County" is, per the Newberry bibliography, the text accompanying the GeorgiaInfo *Historical Atlas of Georgia Counties* map list. No Milton version has `END_N ≥ 19420201`.

1940 Census: same footnotes as above ("Milton and remainder of Campbell annexed to Fulton in 1932"; Table 4 footnote 25 adds "District 1175, formerly in Milton County (population, 933 in 1930; 921 in 1920), consolidated with district 845 in 1932").

Georgia Archives Virtual Vault, collection "Index to County Records on Microfilm (No Images)": items `Milton (Fulton)` and `Milton (Fulton) County` (records 631 and 632; `https://vault.georgiaarchives.org/digital/collection/countycards/id/631`).

Cross-reference (not relied on): New Georgia Encyclopedia *Milton County*: "Milton County, with a population of 6,730 in 1930, merged with Fulton County on January 1, 1932, through an act of the state legislature." Seat Alpharetta.

**Status for the game:** must not appear as a 1942 county. Its territory is the northern lobe of modern Fulton (Alpharetta, Roswell north of the river, Milton, Johns Creek area).

## Fulton County on the reference date (receiving county)

Newberry individual chronology, entry `FULTON`:

> 01 Jan 1932 — FULTON gained all of CAMPBELL (extinct) and MILTON (extinct); CAMPBELL and MILTON eliminated. Acts passed 9 August 1929 and 30 July 1931; both took effect 1 January 1932. By this date, FULTON also gained from COBB. (Ga. Laws 1929, p. 551; Ga. Laws 1931, p. 527; "Milton County," [p. 2])
> by 31 Dec 1986 — FULTON gained small area from CLAYTON (part of lots 6, 7; 13th dist.). (Ga. Dept. Trans., "Atlanta & Vicinity, 1986")

Fulton's Newberry version 7 (in force 1932-01-01 to 1986-12-30) is therefore the 1942 shape. It differs from the modern polygon only by the small 1986 gain from Clayton. Cobb's version 11 (in force from 1932-01-01 through 2000-12-31) is the 1942 shape and is identical to the modern one in Newberry's record.

## Consequences for the roster

- The 159-county roster on 1942-02-01 contains Fulton and Cobb in their post-1932 form and contains neither Campbell nor Milton. This matches the GDD §3.2 requirement that "those former counties must not reappear as separate 1942 starts".
- No county created between 1924 and 1942 exists (Peach, 4 November 1924, was the last creation), and no county other than Campbell and Milton was eliminated at any time after 1812 inside present Georgia.
