## Status

**Bracketed evidence from the 1940 sheet and the 1944 sheet only. No road segment here is verified for 1942-02-01.** No official 1941-1943 State Highway Department map was found (see issue-3-unresolved.md).

Per-county route transcription was not achieved in this synthesis call: the cached tiles cover the state, but tesseract OCR could not read route shields, and image viewing was not available. Every county carries `bracketedRange: "1940-1944"`. Route and `paved` fields are `null` (meaning not transcribed) except where a text source documents a route.

Super Highways 4 lane pavement and over; Paved Roads first class all weather; Improved Roads—second class; Graded Roads; Dirt Roads; Roads Under Construction or contemplated; State Highways; U. S. Interstate Highways; Abbreviations in shield indicate: ALT. = Alternate Route ... Speed Law=55 M.P.H.
- 1944 (rm1944-legend.jpg): U.S. INTERSTATE AND PRINCIPAL STATE HIGHWAYS SHOWN IN RED; Super Highways- 4 lanes or over; Paved Roads first class—all weather; Improved Roads— second class; Graded Roads; Dirt Roads; Roads Under Construction or contemplated; State Highways; U. S. Interstate Highways; ALT. = Alternate; State Forests; Speed Law = 58 m.p.h. (prima facie limit)

Both legends support a later surface-class transcription: Super Highway (4-lane), Paved first class, Improved second class, Graded, Dirt, and Under Construction.

## Cobb-Fulton corridor

Scope: Cobb (US-GA-13067) - Fulton (US-GA-13121) corridor, Marietta-Smyrna-Atlanta

| Designation | What the cache shows |
|---|---|
| US 41 | Documented in NGE Bell Bomber text as a four-lane highway "then under construction" serving Marietta (Dec 1941/early 1942 context). Sheet classification (Super Highway vs Paved first class vs Under Construction) on 1940 and 1944 crops: NOT transcribed. |
| US 41 Alt | NOT evidenced. Both sheet legends define the "ALT." shield abbreviation, but no ALT. shield reading was obtained from the corridor crops. Do not assume an Alt route existed in 1940-1944. |
| State Route 3 | NOT evidenced from cache. OCR of the corridor crops returned no legible SR 3 shield; any US 41/SR 3 concurrency is unverified here. |
| Dixie Highway | Documented by name only in NGE Bell Bomber; no route number attached in the text. |
