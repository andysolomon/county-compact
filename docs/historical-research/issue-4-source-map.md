## Newberry + Rumsey + NGE + USFWS + USGS + Census license caveat

Quoted from [issue-1-newberry-audit.md](issue-1-newberry-audit.md) §3–§4 via [issue-3-source-map.md](issue-3-source-map.md): "The strongest open statement is the Download pages' "reused for any lawful purpose, commercial or non-commercial, without licensing or permission fees to the library" together with the instruction that files containing "the original, out-of-date copyright license … can be ignored". That is a permission statement by the copyright holder, not a CC0 dedication." and "Do not relicense. Do not mark Newberry-derived data as CC0 or public domain." Extension (issue-3-source-map.md): the Rumsey/IA road maps carry an explicit CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 string with a separate "contact … for commercial use" instruction; NGE text is fair-use-only for non-commercial purposes. None of these is CC0; treat all three regimes (Newberry BY-NC-SA 2.5 or "any lawful purpose", Rumsey BY-NC-SA 3.0, NGE non-commercial fair use) as incompatible with commercial distribution until written confirmation is obtained. Modern FIPS codes (`US-GA-<fips>`) are a crosswalk key only; FIPS codes did not exist in 1942.

USGS (re-fetched 2026-09-16, HTTP 200, 81,841 B): "USGS-authored or produced data and information are considered to be in the U.S. Public Domain." USFWS NWI (HTTP 200, 116,322 B): no license sentence on the program page (same observation as issue-3-source-map.md). Census Title 17 sentence is quoted in Facets 1 and 6. New BEA regime encountered: citing page has no public-domain sentence; quoted endorsement/citation lines only (Facet 1).
